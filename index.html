Ikki tur savol

Yes / No · WH questions

Yes / No

Javob qisqa

Do you like tea?

Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.

Ma’lumot so‘ramaydi — tasdiq yoki inkor.

WH question

Javob ma’lumot

What do you like?

I like green tea.

Kim? Nima? Qayerda? — aniq javob kerak.

Xarita

8 so‘z — 8 savol

Who

Person · Kim?

Odam haqida so‘raymiz

Javob — ism, kasb yoki odamni bildiruvchi so‘z.

Who is your teacher?

Mrs Karimova. / My English teacher.

Who do you live with?

I live with my parents.

Who called you yesterday?

My friend called me.

What

Thing / action · Nima?

Narsa yoki ish haqida

Nima? Qanday narsa? Nima qilyapsan?

What is this?

It is a dictionary.

What do you do at weekends?

I play football.

What colour is your bag?

It is dark green.

Where

Place · Qayerda?

Joy va yo‘nalish

Qayerda? Qayerdan? Qayerga?

Where do you live?

I live in Tashkent.

Where is the library?

It is next to the canteen.

Where are you going?

I’m going to school.

When

Time · Qachon?

Vaqt haqida

Soat, kun, oy, fasl, yil.

When is your birthday?

It is on 12 May.

When do lessons start?

They start at 8 o’clock.

When did you arrive?

I arrived yesterday.

Why

Reason · Nega? Nima uchun?

Sabab so‘raymiz

Javob odatda because bilan boshlanadi.

Why are you late?

Because the bus was slow.

Why do you study English?

Because I want to travel.

Why didn’t she come?

Because she was ill.

Which

Choice · Qaysi?

Tanlov ichidan birini

Oldinda 2 yoki undan ko‘p variant bo‘lsa — Which. Umumiy narsa bo‘lsa — What.

Which

Which bag is yours — the blue one or the black one?

Aniq tanlov bor.

What

What colour do you like?

Cheksiz variant. Tanlov berilmagan.

Whose

Possession · Kimniki?

Egalik so‘raymiz

Javob: my, your, Ali’s, hers…

Whose book is this?

It’s Laylo’s book.

Whose phone is ringing?

It’s mine.

Whose parents are teachers?

Jasur’s parents are teachers.

Manner + oilasi · Qanday?

How va uning juftlari

HowQanday?How do you go to school? — By bus.
How oldYoshi?How old are you? — I’m 15.
How manyNechta? (sanoqli)How many books? — Five.
How muchQancha? (sanoqsiz / narx)How much water? / How much is it?
How oftenQanchalik tez-tez?How often do you train? — Twice a week.
How longQancha vaqt / qancha uzun?How long is the film? — Two hours.

WH so‘z yordamchi (do / does / did / is / are / can) ega fe’l

Muhim qoida

Who / What ega bo‘lsa

Savolning o‘zi egaga qaratilgan bo‘lsa, do / does / did qo‘yilmaydi.

Object question

Who did you meet?

Ega = you. Yordamchi kerak: did.

I met the director.

Subject question

Who met the director?

Ega = who. did yo‘q. Fe’l odatdagi shaklda.

Aziza met the director.
